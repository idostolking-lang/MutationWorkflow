# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone who wants to contribute, regardless of
experience, background or identity. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- Being kind, patient and direct. Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for users of the project and for the community.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when an
individual represents the project in public. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow it, and may temporarily or permanently
ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact listed in `SECURITY.md`.
Reports are handled confidentially.
